FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Can I pay when the order arrives?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available on every order, alongside the demo UPI and card options.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 2 to 4 working days. You can follow the status on the My Orders page, from Pending through to Delivered.

What is your returns policy?

Send anything back within 7 days of delivery in its original condition, and we will refund it.

Is my payment information safe?

This demo store never asks for or stores card details at all, which is why checkout only collects a delivery address.
